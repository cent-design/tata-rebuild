FAQs

Do you have a dine-in location?
Tata is a delivery-only kitchen based in Miami, FL — we don’t have a dining room. Order through Uber Eats, DoorDash, or directly through our site and we’ll bring it to you.
What areas do you deliver to?
We deliver across the Miami, FL area. Enter your address on Uber Eats, DoorDash, or our direct ordering page to confirm we deliver to you.
Can I customize my pizza?
Yes — most of our pizzas can be customized with your favorite toppings. Just note your changes at checkout.
How do I place an order?
Order directly through Uber Eats, DoorDash, or our online ordering link — tap any of the buttons above to get started.
