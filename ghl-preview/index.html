About

Founded in Miami, tata is a digital ghost kitchen serving up massive, foldable New York-style pizza made with an ingredient you don't usually find on a standard menu: unconditional love.

We aren't Italian, and we aren't from Brooklyn. tata is named after our grandmother. She didn't grow up tossing dough, but she spent her entire life showing her love through food, feeding anyone who walked through her door. That is the exact energy we are bringing to Miami's pizza scene. Our pies feature crispy, thin crusts, perfectly balanced sauce, and premium mozzarella.

It’s not about checking an authenticity box—it’s about integrity, comfort, and soul. From our single kitchen straight to your couch, we're serving up giant slices with giant hearts.

“The crust is absolute perfection, but it’s the warmth behind the brand that hooks you.”
— A Very Full Miamian

Behind the Slice

Ethos

We believe a great pizza isn’t just baked—it’s a hug in a cardboard box.

At tata, we do things our way. We respect the geometry of a perfect New York slice—the crisp undercarriage, the iconic fold, the perfect cheese-to-sauce ratio—but our secret ingredient comes from a grandmother who taught us that food is the ultimate love language. Everything we serve is made from scratch with patience and intention.

We aren't trying to copy a 100-year-old Neapolitan tradition. We are staying real, keeping it simple, and making sure every single pie that leaves our kitchen tastes like home. No shortcuts, no compromises.

Process

At tata, every pie begins with the dough. Our dough undergoes a slow, cold fermentation process to develop that signature New York chew and crispy, blistered crust. Our tomato sauce is bright, savory, and seasoned perfectly—never rushed.

Because we operate as a ghost kitchen, our entire focus is on the travel-ability of your pizza. We test, measure heat retention, and adjust our bake times so that when the box opens in Downtown or Midtown, the cheese is perfectly melted and the crust is flawlessly crisp.

Supplier List

High-quality pizza requires high-quality basics. We source premium, high-gluten flour for the perfect crust texture, California tomatoes for a sweet and bright sauce base, and the finest whole-milk mozzarella that melts beautifully without getting greasy.

We keep our menu tight and our ingredients fresh so nothing goes to waste and every slice hits the mark.
