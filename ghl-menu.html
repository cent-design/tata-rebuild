Frequently Asked Questions

Reservations & Groups
We do not take reservations and our restaurants are not well suited to big groups. Your best chance is to show up before we open and get in on the first seat, or get ready to be split up!
Dietaries
Our veggie ramen is vegan without the egg. Lots of other dietaries can be catered, we will reply to any other specific dietaries. Please always let us know of any allergies when ordering your food. For the time being we unfortunately cannot accommodate gluten free.
Giftcards
Giftcards can be purchased on the Store section of our website. They can be redeemed at both our restaurants.
Dog & Giraffe Policy
We happily welcome dogs to both our restaurants (and occasionally giraffes - max 3.25m tall).
