About

Founded in Miami, tata is a digital ghost kitchen serving up massive, foldable New York-style pizza made with an ingredient you don't usually find on a standard menu: unconditional love.

We aren't Italian, and we aren't from Brooklyn. tata is named after our grandmother. She didn't grow up tossing dough, but she spent her entire life showing her love through food, feeding anyone who walked through her door. That is the exact energy we are bringing to Miami's pizza scene. Our pies feature crispy, thin crusts, perfectly balanced sauce, and premium mozzarella.

It’s not about checking an authenticity box—it’s about integrity, comfort, and soul. From our single kitchen straight to your couch, we're serving up giant slices with giant hearts.

“The crust is absolute perfection, but it’s the warmth behind the brand that hooks you.”
— A Very Full Miamian
