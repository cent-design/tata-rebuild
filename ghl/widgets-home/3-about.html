Our Story

Founded in Miami, tata is a delivery-only dessert kitchen dedicated to golden, crispy fried Oreos and thick, hand-spun milkshakes. We aren’t a corporate chain, and despite the name, we aren’t Italian—tata is simply what we call our grandmother. She is a woman with an endless amount of love to give, and that exact warmth is the secret ingredient in everything we fry, blend, and scoop.

Our Oreos are fried fresh to order in a sweet, airy batter, and our milkshakes are blended thick with premium local dairy. This isn’t about fancy culinary trends; it’s about comfort, pure nostalgia, and a whole lot of soul.

We’re a tight-knit team serving up treats with heart right here in Miami, operating out of our single high-energy kitchen to deliver pure joy straight to your door.
