About

Founded in Miami, tata is a delivery-only dessert kitchen dedicated to two things: golden, crispy fried Oreos and the thickest, most decadent milkshakes in Florida. We aren’t a corporate chain, and despite the name, we aren’t Italian—tata is simply what we call our grandmother. She is a woman with an endless amount of love to give, and that exact warmth is the secret ingredient in everything we fry, blend, and scoop.

We operate out of a single, high-energy kitchen, whipping up pure nostalgia and delivering it fresh, fast, and still warm right to your doorstep.
