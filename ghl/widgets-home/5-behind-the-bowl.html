Behind the Batter

Our Ethos

We believe great dessert shouldn’t just taste good—it should make you feel safe, happy, and entirely spoiled.

At tata, we do things with heart. We named this concept after our grandmother because she taught us that food is the ultimate love language. She doesn’t care about food trends or fancy culinary pedigree; she cares about feeding people until they smile. We took that exact energy and poured it into deep-fried Oreos and milkshakes.

Everything we serve is made to order with care and intention. Hot, crispy batter. Premium milkshakes hand-spun the second you order. Pure comfort food delivered straight to Miami, Downtown, and Mid-Town.

This isn’t about being a corporate, cookie-cutter dessert chain. It’s about doing things with integrity. Sharing the love. Keeping it sweet. Because if it doesn’t taste like a treat from Tata, it’s not worth serving.

One kitchen. Tight menu. Big love.
No shortcuts, no compromises.

The Melt & The Crisp

At tata, the magic is in the temperature and the texture. We don’t pre-fry anything. The second your order hits our kitchen, the Oreos are dipped in our signature sweet batter and dropped into clean, piping-hot oil, adjusted to ensure perfect fluffiness.

Our milkshakes are hand-spun using premium ice cream and rich dairy to build the ultimate thick, velvety texture. Every single order is timed perfectly so the hot stays crispy, the cold stays frosty, and everything arrives exactly how it’s supposed to.

We don’t cut corners. We fry, spin, and pack until every box hits the mark—simple, indulgent, and uncompromising.

Every order is made fresh, never sitting under a heat lamp, always perfected.

The Good Stuff

Our ingredients come from brands and producers we trust—bringing together the ultimate nostalgic snacks and high-quality local ingredients.

We start with the classics you know and love—like real Nabisco Oreos—and elevate them with premium, locally sourced Florida dairy for our shakes and clean, high-grade oil for our fryers. No heavy, greasy aftertaste here.

It’s simple, we keep it honest, delicious, and delivered fresh to your door. Want to collaborate or bring tata to your next big Miami event? We’d love to hear from you—get in touch with us.
